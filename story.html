Meet Aanchal Khetarpal · Delhi-trained, Auckland-based.

I trained in Vastu in Delhi and have practised for more than five years with families, homes and businesses. After migrating to New Zealand, I returned to first principles and rebuilt my practice around this hemisphere · its light, its seasons and its houses.

What I offer is a careful, older way of asking a modern question: does this home support the people living in it? Most of my recommendations cost nothing to implement. None will ask you to knock down a wall.
