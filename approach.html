Our approach

The universal principles
of a home in harmony.

Vastu Shastra is a holistic design framework centred on the relationship between a building, its occupants and the forces of nature. It brings together the five elements — fire, water, earth, space and air — with orientation, sunlight, airflow and the earth's magnetic field.

At Vastu Designs, these principles are made practical for every background and every kind of home. We study the directions, the site, the local sun path and the way rooms are used, then suggest clear changes that support balance without losing the character of the place.

Responsive to every place

Let the local sun,
air and land lead.

Vastu is applied in response to the local sun path. Here in Aotearoa, the sun tracks through the north, warmth gathers in north-facing rooms, and the cold arrives from the south.

Vastu is not a one-size-fits-all formula. Every recommendation considers the local sun path, prevailing winds, landscape and building patterns, then focuses on practical changes that work with the home you already have.
